PRSS Travel Group

Our PRSS Travel Group started in 2013 as a way for our club to attend the 2014 ScotDance Canadian Championship Series in Vancouver. Living in Northern Alberta, our club wanted to give dancers the experience of travelling together and learning more about highland dance heritage and culture — while making memories with the PRSS dance family. The travel group has since travelled to many locations around the world.

Past Travel Group Experience

  • 2014 — ScotDance Canadian Championship Series, Vancouver
  • 2017 — ScotDance Canadian Championship Series, Prince Edward Island
  • 2023 — Scotland
  • 2025 — Nova Scotia
