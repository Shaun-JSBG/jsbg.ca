Our Classes…

Wee Tots (Stirling)

Ages 3–6 · Saturday mornings, 30 minutes

Wee Tots is our dance program offered to dancers ages 3–6. Classes take place on Saturday mornings and are 30 minutes long. The program is split into two 12-week sessions — a great opportunity for little ones to try highland dance without the commitment of a full year.

Session one runs from September to December. Session two runs from January to the second week in May. Dancers learn basic steps and rhythms; costumes are provided. Wee Tots may participate in community dance-outs and club events like St. Andrews Night and the annual spring recital. New dancers are welcome to join throughout the session.

Aberdeen

Ages 7–10 · New Beginner · 45 min/week

Aberdeen is our new beginner class for dancers who are older than Wee Tots but brand new to the program. Classes focus on the first five dances (Fling, Sword, Seann Truibhas, Lilt & Flora), building technique and musicality. For the 26/27 season, Aberdeen runs Thursdays 5:30–6:15pm.

Paisley

Primary/Beginner · Saturdays, 45 min

Paisley dancers have graduated from Wee Tots. Classes focus on the first five dances, building technique and musicality, with the option to become competitive. For the 26/27 season, Paisley runs Saturdays 10:30–11:15am.

Inverness

Beginner · Weeknights, 60 min

Inverness dancers have gained experience and moved to the weeknight program, continuing to build on the first five dances. For the 26/27 season, classes run Wednesdays 6:30–7:30pm.

Glasgow

Older/experienced · 75 min/week

Glasgow dancers continue the first five dances and learn new dances for exams and competition. Instructors introduce more advanced movement and technique; competitive dancers here are beginner or novice level. For the 26/27 season, classes run Thursdays 5:30–6:45pm.

Edinburgh

Advanced · 90 min/week

Edinburgh is for advanced dancers — Intermediate and Premier level competitors, and dancers at minimum gold exam level starting highland theory. Classes build strength and technical ability and introduce new steps for exams and competition. For the 26/27 season, classes run Thursdays 6:45–8:15pm.

Technique

Saturdays, 75 min

Open to Inverness, Glasgow, and Edinburgh dancers, focused on strength and technique specifically for competition.

Choreography

Monthly, Saturdays

For older dancers year-round, and younger dancers January–May. Focused on choreography used in club performances and competitions; dancers must be able to commit to practices. Speak with instructors if your dancer is interested.
