Peace River Scottish Society

Our club delivers high-quality highland dance lessons from dedicated and experienced instructors. We believe highland dancing creates a lifelong journey of friendship and lasting memories.

The PRSS dance program fosters hard work, personal growth and fun — with a place for everyone, competitive and recreational alike.
